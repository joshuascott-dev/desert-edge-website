What We Do

Our Services

From utility work to expansion joints, Desert Edge delivers precise, professional cutting solutions tailored to Arizona's demanding conditions.

01 🪨
Concrete Saw Cutting

Flat slab, green concrete, and decorative cutting for floors, driveways, slabs, and hardscapes. Diamond blade precision.

02 🛣️
Asphalt Cutting

Road repairs, utility patches, and lane markings. We cut clean edges that minimize asphalt damage and speed up restoration.

03 ⭕
Concrete Coring

Diamond core drilling for plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and anchor penetrations through slabs, walls, and decks. Clean, round holes from small diameters up to large-bore openings.

04 🕳️
Manhole & Vault Coring

Core drilling pipe penetrations into manholes, vaults, and below-grade structures for new utility connections, including tight trench and confined-space work.

05 🔲
Control Joint Cutting

Precision joint placement to prevent random cracking in concrete slabs—done at the right time and depth.

06 💧
Utility Trenching

Straight, clean trenches for water, gas, fiber, and electrical utilities—minimizing disturbance to surrounding surfaces.

07 🏗️
Commercial Projects

Warehouses, parking lots, loading docks, and large-scale commercial slabs. We handle big footprints efficiently.

08 🏠
Residential Cutting

Pool decks, driveways, walkways, and garage floors. Clean work with minimal mess and disruption to your property.
